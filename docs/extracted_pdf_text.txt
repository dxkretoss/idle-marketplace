01
Project overview and understanding
IDLE is a social-first local marketplace built on a simple idea: no one and nothing should have
to sit idle. It connects neighbors and local businesses for rentals, services and local
opportunities.
Members can rent out unused tools, goods and space, offer their skills and time, and find trusted help
nearby. IDLE earns recurring revenue from membership subscriptions rather than taking a commission
on transactions between members. The planned launch market is a controlled pilot in the Dallas–Fort
Worth area.

What this proposal covers. The complete IDLE platform with all core features: web application, iOS
and Android apps, shared backend and admin portal, including social Reels, member payments and
deposits, identity verification, QR handoffs, subscriptions, analytics, AI support, enterprise operations
and a third-party tools marketplace. Later enhancements are listed as future scope.
02
Proposed development approach
We will build a production-quality platform in clear milestones, with a working demo at the end of each
one.
1
Start from the existing design
We will review and polish IDLE's current concept designs and brand, not redesign from scratch.
2
Agree on scope before building
The final feature list and acceptance criteria are confirmed in writing before development begins.
3
Build once, ship everywhere
One shared backend and one cross-platform codebase for iOS and Android, with shared design and
logic packages in a single repository.
4
Keep important rules on the server
Payments, subscription access and data permissions are enforced by the backend, so they cannot be
bypassed through the apps.
5
Hand over a platform the client owns
Source code, accounts and documentation stay under Luna Enterprises' control.
